Bill's Best Bets for the Weekend

Weekend of November 14–15, 2026 · Drive times from San Francisco
Best bet this weekend

Lower Sacramento, Redding

Fishing has stabilized, with trout eating midges and Blue Wing Olives as the Baetis hatch. Eggs still work, but BWO nymphs and rubber legs are the main producers. Steelhead are showing up in greater numbers below Jellys Ferry. The first storm of the season is expected this weekend and may color the water.

Flies
Nymphs: BP Weiss Nymph, small Olive Hot Spot #18, Jigged Birds Nest #14–16 (natural or hot spot), Fox's Poopah #12–14, amber Bubbleback Caddis #14, Peaches n' Green, Peaches n' Cream, Ginger Snap, Pat's Rubber Legs #4–8. Eggs: Micro Spawn or Boles Bazookas in Peachy King or Shrimp Pink. Dries: dark Mercer's Missing Link, Spotlight Caddis Emerger, BWO or PMD Comparadun.
Drive
About 3½–4 hours
Flow
About 5,200 cfs below Keswick Dam; about 6,500 cfs above Bend Bridge
Weather
Saturday mostly cloudy with a 40% chance of rain, mainly before 10 am, high near 57°F; Sunday rain (90% chance), high near 54°F
Access
Public wading spots in Redding and Anderson at flows below about 7,500 cfs; most water is fished from a drift boat
Rules
Barbless hooks. Wild trout and wild steelhead must be released.
Full report: The Fly Shop, Nov 11 ›
Trout on the egg bite

Lower Yuba River

Reported in good shape and fishing well, with a tint in the water. Salmon are on active redds from the bridge down to Daguerre and trout are eating eggs throughout; nymphing is most productive. Dry-fly fishing is sparse and limited to the flats.

Flies
Egg patterns, olive caddis, rubber legs and smaller mayfly nymphs (no sizes named in the report). Hatches depend on weather: caddis and larger mayflies on warm, sunny days; Baetis and Tricos in the warmest part of the day.
Drive
About 2½ hours (Marysville area)
Flow
About 560 cfs near Marysville; the Nov 3 report put the river around 1,000 cfs
Weather
Saturday partly sunny, high near 63°F; Sunday rain, mainly after 4 pm (80% chance), high near 61°F
Access
Sycamore Ranch, Hammon Grove, and the hike-in on the south side of the Highway 20 bridge
Rules
Closed above the Highway 20 bridge until December 1. Below it: artificial lures, barbless hooks, catch and release. No salmon fishing; stay off spawning beds.
Full report: Fly Fishing Specialties (Capt. Hogan Brown), Nov 3 ›
Steelhead close to home

Lower American River

Clear, rated fair to good. The salmon run is near its peak, and half-pounders sit just downstream of the spawning beds eating eggs.

Flies
Pale pink egg patterns #12 below spawning beds; Bird's Nest #12–14, Caddis Poopah #12–14, olive Micro Mayfly #18, black Rubber Legs #8–14, Prince Nymph #12–14, tan SJ Worm #12; Woolly Bugger #10 in black, olive or rusty for swinging.
Drive
About 1¾ hours (Sacramento)
Flow
About 1,210 cfs at Fair Oaks, water near 57°F
Weather
Saturday mostly sunny, high near 63°F; Sunday rain likely, mainly after 4 pm (60% chance), high near 63°F
Access
County parks below Ancil Hoffman, such as River Bend, William B. Pond and Watt Avenue
Rules
Closed from below Nimbus Hatchery down to the power lines at Ancil Hoffman Park through December 31. Steelhead Report Card required.
Full report: California Fly Fishing Reports, Oct 26 ›
Closest trout water

Putah Creek

Low and clear, rated good. Flows have dropped enough that wading is easy; small nymphs are the program.

Flies
#18–22 nymphs: Zebra Midge or WD-40 in olive, gray, black or red; Perdigons in olive, red or black; Flashback Pheasant Tail. Streamers along steep banks if flows rise.
Drive
About 1¼ hours (Winters, Highway 128)
Flow
About 100 cfs near Winters
Weather
Saturday patchy morning fog, then mostly sunny, high near 63°F; Sunday rain, mainly after 10 am (80% chance), high near 61°F
Access
Five Yolo County fishing access sites along Highway 128 below Monticello Dam
Rules
Artificial flies and lures, barbless hooks, catch and release all year. Many anglers stay off the creek December 1 to March 1 for spawning.
Full report: California Fly Fishing Reports, Oct 25 ›
No long drive

Bay Area Stripers

Stripers are spread through the Bay, with the East Bay (Alameda, Berkeley Bulb, Point Molate) strongest and Crissy Field improving. The water has been very clean, and lighter colors have done better. This is the newest Bay report available.

Flies
Lighter-colored patterns in chartreuse, olive or gray (no specific Bay pattern named). For the Delta, 3/0 Clousers with a rattle.
Drive
0–30 minutes (Crissy Field, Alameda, Point Molate)
Flow
Tides at San Francisco: Saturday high 5.2 ft at 1:34 pm, low 0.1 ft at 8:48 pm; Sunday high 4.9 ft at 2:27 pm, low 0.3 ft at 9:39 pm
Weather
Saturday mostly cloudy, becoming partly cloudy, with a chance of rain, highs in the lower 60s; Sunday rain, highs in the lower 60s
Access
Public shorelines: Crissy Field in San Francisco; Alameda and Point Molate in the East Bay
Rules
Striped bass size and bag limits apply; check current rules.
