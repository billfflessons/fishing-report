Bill's Best Bets for the Weekend

Weekend of November 28–29, 2026 · Drive times from San Francisco
Best bet this weekend

Lower Sacramento, Redding

Rain on Nov 18 added some color, but visibility was five to six feet and the fish were biting. Trout are eating midges and Blue Wing Olives; with Baetis hatching, BWO nymphs and rubber legs are the main producers, and eggs still take some fish. Steelhead are expected in greater numbers below Jellys Ferry. Keswick releases have since come down to about 3,000 cfs. The report notes storms can cloud the water below Clear Creek and the other major tributaries, and rain is forecast for Sunday.

Flies
Nymphs: BP Weiss Nymph #16–18, Olive Hot Spot #18, Jigged Birds Nest #14–16 (natural or hot spot), Fox's Poopah #12–14, amber Bubbleback Caddis #14, Pat's Rubber Legs #4–8; also Peaches n' Green, Peaches n' Cream and Ginger Snap (no size given). Eggs: Micro Spawn or Boles Bazookas in Peachy King or Shrimp Pink (no size given). Dries: dark Mercer's Missing Link, Spotlight Caddis Emerger, BWO or PMD Comparadun (no size given).
Drive
About 3½–4 hours
Flow
About 3,000 cfs below Keswick Dam (the report was written as releases were being lowered from 5,000 cfs); about 5,600 cfs above Bend Bridge
Weather
Saturday mostly cloudy, high near 66°F; Sunday rain likely, mainly after 10 am, high near 57°F
Access
Public wading spots in Redding and Anderson at flows below about 7,500 cfs; the report calls a drift boat the best way to fish it
Rules
Barbless hooks. Wild trout and wild steelhead must be released.

Early steelhead showing

Lower American River

Rated fair to good. Fall Chinook are actively spawning, especially between William B. Pond and Gristmill, and steelhead are feeding on drifting eggs. Early winter steelhead are starting to show.

Flies
Nymphs and eggs: Bird's Nest #12–14, egg patterns #12, Caddis Poopah #12–14, olive Micro Mayfly #18, black Rubber Legs #8–14, Prince Nymph #12–14, tan San Juan Worm #12. Swing: Woolly Buggers #10 (black, olive, rusty), Slump Busters #10, Zonkers #10, Silver Hiltons and Intruders (no size given). Dries for half-pounders: Parachute Adams #14–18, Elk Hair Caddis #14–16, Olive Parachute #18 (BWO), Hackle Stacker Baetis #18–20.
Drive
About 1¾ hours (Fair Oaks and Rancho Cordova)
Flow
About 1,200 cfs at Fair Oaks, water about 56°F
Weather
Saturday partly sunny, high near 63°F; Sunday rain, mainly 10 am to 4 pm, then showers and possibly a thunderstorm (80% chance), high near 63°F
Access
American River Parkway access below Ancil Hoffman Park, including Gristmill and William B. Pond
Rules
Closed from below Nimbus Dam to the Ancil Hoffman Park power lines, November 1 to December 31. Check current regulations for the open water below.
Full report: California Fly Fishing Reports, Nov 20 ›
Trout on the egg bite

Lower Yuba River

Fishing has been strong for several weeks, with tinted water. Salmon are still spawning, so trout are eating fresh and dead eggs, with bugs mixed in from mid-morning. Dry-fly fish have been sipping spent Tricos. If rain from Deer Creek blows the river out, it is reported to clear quickly. This is the newest Yuba guide report available.

Flies
Subsurface: rubber legs, San Juan Worms, caddis, Prince Nymphs and Perdigon attractors; egg patterns while salmon spawn. Dries: Parachute Adams, crippled caddis and pinkies. The report names no sizes or colors, and no other recent Yuba report gives sizes for these patterns.
Drive
About 2½ hours (Marysville area)
Flow
About 850 cfs below Englebright Dam and about 600 cfs near Marysville; the Nov 13 report put the river around 1,000 cfs with a tint
Weather
Saturday partly sunny, high near 62°F; Sunday a chance of rain, then showers and possibly a thunderstorm after 10 am (90% chance), high near 60°F
Access
Sycamore Ranch, Hammon Grove, and the hike-in on the south side of the Highway 20 bridge
Rules
Closed above the Highway 20 bridge until December 1. Below it: artificial lures, barbless hooks, catch and release. No salmon fishing; stay off spawning beds.
Full report: Fly Fishing Specialties (Capt. Ben Thompson), Nov 13 ›
Closest trout water

Putah Creek

Flows had dropped to about 104 cfs and clear, making wading easy, and fish were found with few anglers out in the morning. Nymphs under a double or triple indicator rig produced, and the report says any nymph in sizes #18–22 will work. This is the newest Putah report available; this is the last weekend before many anglers stay off the creek for spawning.

Flies
Zebra Midges and WD-40s (olive, gray, black or red), Perdigons (olive, red or black) and Flashback Pheasant Tails, all #18–22 per the report; other mayfly patterns smaller than #18.
Drive
About 1¼ hours (Winters, Highway 128)
Flow
About 100 cfs near Winters, close to the flow in the report
Weather
Saturday partly sunny, high near 62°F; Sunday rain, mainly 10 am to 4 pm, then showers and possibly a thunderstorm (90% chance), high near 58°F
Access
Five Yolo County fishing access sites along Highway 128 below Monticello Dam
Rules
Artificial flies and lures, barbless hooks, catch and release all year. Many anglers stay off the creek December 1 to March 1 for spawning.
Full report: California Fly Fishing Reports, Oct 25 ›
No long drive

Bay Area Stripers

Stripers are spread through the Bay, with the East Bay (Alameda, Berkeley Bulb, Point Molate) strongest and Crissy Field improving. The water has been warm and very clean, and lighter colors have done better. This is the newest Bay report available.

Flies
Lighter-colored patterns in chartreuse, olive or gray. Lost Coast's Oct 13 SF Bay report (Brett Wedeking) named Adachi Clousers #2–1/0 in chartreuse, Joker and surf brown. For the Delta, 3/0 Clousers with a rattle.
Drive
0–30 minutes (Crissy Field, Alameda, Point Molate)
Flow
Tides at San Francisco: Saturday high 4.9 ft at 3:09 am, low 3.2 ft at 7:31 am, high 6.2 ft at 1:28 pm, low −1.0 ft at 8:30 pm; Sunday high 5.0 ft at 4:03 am, low 3.0 ft at 8:49 am, high 5.6 ft at 2:32 pm, low −0.5 ft at 9:27 pm
Weather
Saturday partly sunny, high near 63–65°F; Sunday rain (90% chance at the shoreline), high near 63°F
Access
Public shorelines: Crissy Field in San Francisco; Alameda and Point Molate in the East Bay
Rules
Striped bass size and bag limits apply; check current rules.
