Bill's Best Bets for the Weekend

Weekend of November 7–8, 2026 · Drive times from San Francisco
Best bet this weekend

Lower Yuba River

Fishing well with a little color in the water. Salmon are spawning from the bridge down, so trout are on eggs; nymphing has been best, dries are sparse.

Flies
Egg patterns (Glo Bug or bead) wherever salmon are spawning; olive caddis pupa, rubber legs, small Baetis nymphs. A few October Caddis on warm afternoons.
Drive
About 2½ hours (Marysville area)
Flow
About 1,060 cfs below Englebright Dam; 560 cfs near Marysville
Weather
Sunny Saturday, mostly sunny Sunday, highs near 72°F
Access
Sycamore Ranch, Hammon Grove, and the hike-in on the south side of the Highway 20 bridge
Rules
Closed above the Highway 20 bridge until December 1. Below it: artificial lures, barbless hooks, catch and release. No salmon fishing; stay off spawning beds.

Closest trout water

Putah Creek

Low and clear, rated good. Flows have dropped enough that wading is easy at every access point; the smaller the fly, the better.

Flies
#18–22 nymphs on a double or triple indicator rig: Zebra Midge or WD-40 in olive, gray, black or red; Perdigons in olive, red or black; Flashback Pheasant Tail.
Drive
About 1¼ hours (Winters, Highway 128)
Flow
About 100 cfs
Weather
Sunny, highs 72–74°F
Access
Five Yolo County fishing access sites along Highway 128 below Monticello Dam
Rules
Artificial flies and lures, barbless hooks, catch and release all year. Many anglers stay off the creek December 1 to March 1 for spawning.

Steelhead close to home

Lower American River

Clear, rated fair to good. The salmon run is near its peak, and half-pounders sit just downstream of the spawning beds eating eggs.

Flies
Pale pink egg patterns #12 below spawning beds; Bird's Nest #12–14, Caddis Poopah #12–14, olive Micro Mayfly #18, black Rubber Legs #8–14, Prince Nymph #12–14; Woolly Bugger #10 in black, olive or rusty for swinging.
Drive
About 1¾ hours (Sacramento)
Flow
About 1,220 cfs at Fair Oaks, water near 58°F
Weather
Sunny Saturday, mostly sunny Sunday, highs near 72°F
Access
County parks below Ancil Hoffman, such as River Bend, William B. Pond and Watt Avenue
Rules
Closed from below Nimbus Hatchery down to the power lines at Ancil Hoffman Park from November 1. Steelhead Report Card required.
Full report: California Fly Fishing Reports, Oct 26 ›
Worth the drive

Lower Sacramento, Redding

The fall-run kings are finishing up and the river has been dropped. The Posse-to-Bonnyview float still gets heavy pressure; guides say the water below Anderson is fishing very well. Steelhead are showing up in greater numbers below Jellys Ferry.

Flies
Egg patterns where salmon are spawning; otherwise BWO nymphs and Rubber Legs. Best: BP Weiss Nymph, small Olive Hot Spot, and anything small and green.
Drive
About 3½–4 hours
Flow
About 5,100 cfs below Keswick Dam
Weather
Sunny Saturday, partly sunny Sunday, highs near 70–72°F
Access
Public wading spots in Redding and Anderson at these flows; most water is fished from a drift boat
Rules
Barbless hooks. Wild trout and wild steelhead must be released.
Full report: The Fly Shop, Nov 4 ›
No long drive

Bay Area Stripers

Stripers are moving through the Bay. Lighter-colored flies have done better in clear water; cover water, the fish keep moving.

Flies
Light-colored baitfish patterns in chartreuse, olive or gray (no specific pattern named in the report).
Drive
0–30 minutes (Crissy Field, Alameda, Point Molate)
Flow
Tides, not flows: check a tide chart
Weather
Partly sunny, highs 63–64°F, light west wind
Access
Public shorelines: Crissy Field in San Francisco; Alameda and Point Molate in the East Bay
Rules
Striped bass size and bag limits apply; check current rules.
